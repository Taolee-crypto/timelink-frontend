Creator Policy

Effective: March 16, 2026 · v1.0

Who Is a Creator?

A creator is a user who contributes digital content such as music, video, documents and images to TimeLink and earns rewards from its use. To get started, share an eligible digital asset through SharePlace.

Reward Sharing Plans

Plan A — Standard
Focused on immediate cash rewards
TL_P usage share: 62% received
TL_A usage share: 50% received (non-convertible)
Conversion fee 5%
Base TLC mining multiplier (1.0×)
Plan B — Growth
Designed to maximize TLC mining
TL_P usage share: 45% received
TL_A usage share: 35% received (non-convertible)
Conversion fee 3%
TLC mining 2× bonus
You can change your plan at any time in Creator Center.

TL Conversion (Cash Withdrawal)

  • Minimum conversion amount: 10,000 TL_P
  • Plan A: KRW is paid after a 5% conversion fee.
  • Plan B: KRW is paid after a 3% conversion fee.
  • Settlement: monthly, at the end of each month, by transfer to the registered bank account.
  • TL_A and TL_B cannot be converted.

Prohibited Content

  • Content that infringes another party’s copyright, including unauthorized sampling or plagiarism.
  • Commercial use of AI-generated music without the required license.
  • Pornographic, hateful or violent content.
  • Content containing false or fraudulent information.
  • Content that violates another person’s privacy.

Uploading prohibited content may result in removal, forfeiture of rewards and account suspension.

Channel Operations (Cafe & In-Car Broadcast)

  • Channel setup fee: 50,000 TL (renews monthly).
  • Creator reward sharing also applies to content played through the channel.
  • In-Car listening while driving: 2.5× POC multiplier.
  • Unused TL balances are not refunded when a channel is closed.
