Article 4. Disclosure to Third Parties

As a rule, the Company does not disclose users’ personal information to external parties. Exceptions include:

  • When the user has given prior consent.
  • When disclosure is required by law or by a lawful request from an investigative authority.
  • When minimum necessary information is provided to PortOne (formerly I’mport) or Stripe to process payments.
  • When anonymized data is provided to Google to operate advertising services using the Google IMA SDK.

Article 6. User Rights

Users may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion, except where retention is required by law.
  • Request suspension of processing.

Requests may be sent to privacy@timelink.digital and will be handled within 10 business days.

For privacy complaints in Korea: Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr · dial 118).

Article 7. Cookies and Automatic Collection

Cookies are used to maintain sign-in status and store preferences such as language settings. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some service features may become unavailable.
