⚖️ Copyright Protection Policy

Legal Notice

The uploader is solely responsible for the legal status of all content submitted to TimeLink. Copyright infringement may result in civil and criminal liability.

1. Verification System

TimeLink uses AI-based similarity checks as one part of its content-protection process. Certification and rights status are determined through the applicable review process.

✅ Certified Content
  • AI OCR verification completed (profile, payment or creation evidence)
  • AI similarity review passed (similarity below 70%)
  • Verification linked to official platforms such as Spotify
  • Manual verification by an authorized administrator
❌ Unverified Content
  • The uploader remains responsible for all legal obligations.
  • Content may be removed immediately after a valid infringement report.
  • Repeated violations may result in permanent account suspension.

2. AI Similarity Review

Uploaded audio and video may be analyzed by AI for similarity to existing works.

  • Below 30%: Lower similarity range (may be eligible for verification)
  • 30–70%: Review required (manual review may be needed)
  • 70% or higher: High-risk range (submission may be blocked)

3. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you identify content that may infringe your rights, please report it promptly.
