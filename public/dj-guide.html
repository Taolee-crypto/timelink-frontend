☕What Is Cafe Radio?
DJ가 큐레이션한 TL3 음악을 카페 매장에서 방송하는 시스템입니다.
DJ는 방송을 진행하고, 카페는 매장에서 그 방송을 재생합니다.
🎧
DJ
  • Select and curate TL3 music
  • Broadcast live or use automated playback
  • Broadcast to participating cafes
  • Receive a 50% share of eligible broadcast usage
☕
Cafe
  • Match with DJs (application → approval)
  • Play broadcasts in the cafe
  • Cafe owner’s TL is used for listening
  • Enhance the in-store atmosphere
🔄How It Works
The process from applying to a cafe to broadcasting and receiving rewards.
1
Create a DJ Profile — Add your DJ name, genres and bio in DJ Center.
2
Apply to a Cafe — Send an application to a cafe you want to broadcast for.
3
Cafe Approval — The broadcast contract begins when the cafe owner accepts.
4
Start Broadcasting — Broadcast TL3 music through Cafe Radio.
5
Rewards Accrue — TL used by the cafe owner is shared with the DJ.

⚠️Broadcasting Rules
Rules that must be followed when broadcasting on Cafe Radio.
⛔ Source MP3 Files Cannot Be Broadcast
  • Source MP3 files cannot be broadcast on Cafe Radio.
  • Legacy MP3 entries are excluded from the broadcast queue.
  • This restriction protects rights management and reward distribution.
✅ TL3 Assets Only
  • Only TL3 digital assets can be broadcast on Cafe Radio.
  • TL3 supports rights protection and transparent reward distribution.
  • In Creator Center, select a source MP3 on your computer to create a TL3 asset. The source remains on your computer.

❓Frequently Asked Questions
How do I become a DJ?
Create a profile in DJ Center and apply to cafes you want to broadcast for. You can start once a cafe owner accepts your application.
When and how do DJs receive rewards?
Eligible TL usage generated by listening is recorded for DJ rewards. You can review your activity in DJ Center.
Can I broadcast an MP3 file?
No. Only TL3 digital assets can be broadcast. Select a source MP3 on your computer in Creator Center to create a TL3 asset; the original stays on your computer.
Can I have contracts with multiple cafes?
Yes. You can broadcast for multiple cafes. Each cafe contract is managed separately.
How do DJ bonuses work?
Eligible milestones may trigger TL_B rewards automatically. Examples include your first broadcast, one hour of broadcasting and listener milestones.
